QUESTION:
Some people believe that university education should be free for all students, while others think students should pay for it. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

ESSAY:
In todays world, education is very important for every person. Some people think that university should be free for everyone, but other people believe that students must pay the fees. In this essay I will discuss both views and give my opinion.

On the one hand, free university education have many advantages. Firstly, it give chance to poor students who cannot afford the fees. For example, in Germany most universities are free, so students from every background can study. Also, when more people are educated, the country get more doctors, engineers and teachers, which is good for the economy. Without free education, many talented students would loose their opportunity only because of money.

On the other hand, there are people who think that students should pay by themselves. They argue that government have limited budget and the money can be used for hospitals and roads. In addition, if students pay, they will take their study more seriously and not waste time. I think this is a reasonable point, because things which are free are often not valued.

In my opinion, the best solution is a mix of both. The government should pay a large part of the fees, but students should also pay a small amount, or get loans which they return after getting a job. In this way, poor students can still study and the burden on government is reduced.

To conclude, both views have good points. I believe that education is a investment for the future, so government should support students but not completely.
